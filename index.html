Privacy Policy — Yupoo Gallery Downloader Last updated: 2026-09-22 Yupoo Gallery Downloader is a Chrome extension that downloads images and videos from Yupoo gallery pages you already have open, saving them to your own computer's Downloads folder. What this extension does NOT do: - It does not track your browsing activity. - It does not collect or transmit your Yupoo username, password, or account details. - It does not read or transmit the content of any page other than Yupoo gallery pages. - Downloaded files are never uploaded to any server — they go straight to your Downloads folder. What this extension DOES send off your device: - If you activate a paid license, the license key you enter in Settings is sent to our licensing server (a Cloudflare Worker) solely to check whether it is currently valid. No other information accompanies this request. - Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Data we store: - Your resize/rename settings and cached license status are stored locally in your browser via Chrome's storage API. This stays on your device (and syncs only through your own Google/Chrome account, like any other extension setting). - If you purchase a license, our licensing database stores: your license key, the email address collected by Stripe at checkout, and your Stripe customer/subscription ID — used solely to verify subscription status. This data is not used for marketing and is not sold to third parties. Contact: yupoogallerydownloader@gmail.com This policy may be updated; changes will be reflected on this page.
